# AI Engineer Associate

**Trinity Industries** is looking for an **AI Engineer Associate** for our office in **Dallas, TX**. You will build and deploy agentic AI applications that business teams across the enterprise use daily, working with large language models, agent frameworks, and AI\-assisted coding. You will take solutions from prototype through production within the team's established architecture, code review, deployment, and governance practices, with guidance from experienced engineers.

**What You'll Do:**

* Design, prototype, and deliver agentic systems that plan and execute multi\-step business tasks, including the connectors and integrations to internal systems and APIs they depend on.
* Implement agent controls as a core part of the build: evaluation against defined success criteria, scoped and secure tool access, human approval steps where actions carry business or financial risk, and monitoring of agent behavior in production.
* Produce code and deployable artifacts using AI\-assisted generation, and read, explain, test, and troubleshoot that code before it ships.
* Deploy and support applications through the team's source control, automated testing, and CI/CD pipelines — including triaging issues and iterating after launch.
* Apply data governance and access\-control practices to every solution you build, in partnership with data engineering.
* Collaborate with data analysts, data scientists, data engineers, and business stakeholders to gain in\-depth knowledge of business problems and likely solutions.
* Demo results and deliver concise handoff docs, runbooks, and impact metrics.


**What You'll Have:**

* Bachelor's degree in computer science, engineering, a quantitative discipline, or equivalent demonstrated practical experience.
* Demonstrable experience building AI prototypes or agentic projects (internship, coursework, research, personal projects, or employment).
* Ability to generate code using AI\-assisted coding tools in an IDE and to read, explain, test, and troubleshoot what those tools produce.
* Familiarity with SQL and cloud data platforms (Databricks, Azure, AWS, or equivalent).
* Working knowledge of version control (Git) and collaborative development practices.
* Strong problem solving and communication skills; ability to present technical work to nontechnical stakeholders.
* Ability to manage multiple concurrent projects and communicate status without close supervision.

**Preferred / Nice\-to\-Have**

* Experience working with unstructured text and retrieval approaches (RAG or index \+ LLM patterns) for document/question answering.
* Comfortable integrating services via REST APIs and using secure authentication patterns.
* Exposure to CI/CD tooling (Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions, or similar) and deploying applications through automated pipelines.
* Experience building or working with data/workflow pipelines or orchestration tools, or familiarity with the concepts of scheduling and task orchestration.
* Exposure to observability practices for deployed applications: logging, cost tracking, and basic drift checks.
* Exposure to data governance concepts such as catalogs, lineage, and role\-based access control.

**Growth Path**


This is an entry\-level role with opportunity to progress into a more senior individual contributor role. You will work on enterprise platforms (Databricks and Palantir Foundry) using enterprise\-provided AI development tools and take on increasing ownership across the application lifecycle starting within established patterns and moving toward leading projects end\-to\-end as your experience grows.
