# Toyota North America

## Overview

Toyota Motor North America (TMNA) coordinates Toyota’s North American manufacturing, research and development, sales, marketing, and other operations. It is headquartered in Plano, Texas. Toyota says it directly employs **nearly 64,000 people across North America**; that figure covers its regional operations, not just the Plano office. The Lead ML/AI Engineer posting is for **Toyota Financial Services (TFS)**, a separate Toyota business entity. ([global.toyota](https://global.toyota/en/company/profile/facilities/manufacturing-worldwide/north_america.html?utm_source=openai))

## Products and Services

Toyota’s regional operations develop, manufacture, and sell Toyota and Lexus vehicles. TFS provides vehicle financing and leasing, protection products, and auto insurance offerings for customers and dealers. ([global.toyota](https://global.toyota/en/company/profile/facilities/manufacturing-worldwide/north_america.html?utm_source=openai))

## Funding and Ownership

TMNA is part of **Toyota Motor Corporation**, a publicly traded company, rather than a venture-backed startup. Toyota identifies its shares under securities code 7203 in Japan and NYSE ticker TM. No venture funding round is applicable to TMNA. ([global.toyota](https://global.toyota/en/company/profile/facilities/manufacturing-worldwide/north_america.html?utm_source=openai))

## AI Focus

Toyota has an enterprise AI organization responsible for AI strategy, governance, delivery, and adoption. Its Global AI Accelerator is intended to expand AI research and implementation across areas including customer relations, business software, manufacturing, and vehicle engineering. ([pressroom.toyota.com](https://pressroom.toyota.com/biographies/brian-kursar/?utm_source=openai))

**This particular role is focused on TFS products, not autonomous driving.** The posting describes a hands-on lead engineer building production ML systems on AWS: data ingestion and feature engineering, model training and evaluation, deployment, testing, and monitoring. It also calls for integrating large language models into product features using prompting, retrieval-augmented generation, and agent-based approaches. The engineer would choose among foundation models through Amazon Bedrock, custom training on SageMaker, and conventional ML; address data quality, inference latency, and model drift; and mentor other engineers. These are responsibilities stated in the posting, not a claim that every listed feature is already deployed. ([cvrocket.ai](https://cvrocket.ai/jobs/toyota-north-america/lead-ml-ai-engineer-7108acccab96?utm_source=openai))
