# Alvarez & Marsal

## Overview

Alvarez & Marsal (A&M) is a global consulting firm headquartered in New York. It advises organizations on business performance, restructuring and transformation. A&M reports **more than 13,500 employees**. Its own careers site lists the Senior Manager, AI Engineer Intelligence Layer role in Chicago. ([alvarezandmarsal.com](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/about-am))

## Products and Services

A&M provides advisory, business performance improvement and turnaround management services to companies, investors and government entities. Its offerings also include transaction advisory, private equity services, and artificial intelligence and analytics consulting. ([alvarezandmarsal.com](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/about-am))

## Funding and Ownership

A&M describes itself as **privately held since its founding in 1983**. No reliable information on notable funding rounds was found. ([linkedin.com](https://www.linkedin.com/company/alvarez-%26-marsal/))

## AI Focus

A&M uses AI both in client work and in its internal operations. Its AI and Analytics practice offers AI strategy, predictive analytics, generative AI, workflow automation and AI governance. In May 2026, the firm announced a Global AI Board to coordinate how AI is incorporated into client delivery and scaled responsibly across the firm. ([alvarezandmarsal.com](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/expertise/artificial-intelligence-and-analytics-aia))

The **Chicago role is focused on A&M’s internal Intelligence Layer**, rather than solely on client consulting. The job posting describes it as a knowledge-graph platform intended to make approved structured and unstructured firm knowledge securely searchable across the organization and within business units. The engineer would help build ingestion pipelines, graph models, retrieval services, APIs and user-facing features, using technologies such as Python, Neo4j and Cypher. The role also covers integrations with large language models and the Model Context Protocol, plus production agentic workflows with evaluation, guardrails, observability and human oversight. Preserving source permissions and client confidentiality is an explicit engineering requirement. ([careers.alvarezandmarsal.com](https://careers.alvarezandmarsal.com/jobs/18274028-senior-manager-ai-engineer-intelligence-layer))
