# Optum

## Overview
Optum is a UnitedHealth Group healthcare business headquartered in Eden Prairie, Minnesota. It provides care and technology-enabled services to patients and healthcare organizations, and says it has roughly **300,000 employees worldwide**. The specified AI/ML Engineer posting is on UnitedHealth Group’s careers site and describes the team as Optum Tech; the posting lists its organization as **UHC Medicare & Retirement**. ([optum.com](https://www.optum.com/en/about-us.html))

## Products and Services
Optum’s principal businesses are **Optum Health**, which provides patient care and care-management services; **Optum Insight**, which provides healthcare data, analytics, technology and consulting; and **Optum Rx**, which provides pharmacy care services. ([sec.gov](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176626000062/unh-20251231.htm))

## Funding and Ownership
Optum is part of **UnitedHealth Group**, rather than a separately listed company. UnitedHealth Group is publicly traded on the New York Stock Exchange under **UNH**. Optum’s ownership by a public parent is the relevant funding context; no separate venture-funding round is documented in the sources used here. ([optum.com](https://www.optum.com/en/about-us.html))

## AI Focus
Optum uses data and technology across healthcare delivery and operations, while Optum Insight offers analytics and technology to healthcare organizations. The specified opening gives a more concrete picture of the engineering work: it calls for building machine-learning solutions using **large language models**, working with open- and closed-source models through **LangChain**, and applying **fine-tuning** and **retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)** for particular tasks and datasets. ([sec.gov](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176626000062/unh-20251231.htm))

The posting also lists traditional ML work, including **XGBoost classification** and **K-means clustering**. An engineer in this role would be expected to write production-quality Python, test and document code, and use Docker for development and deployment. The posting does **not** identify a specific product or clinical use case for this position, so those responsibilities should not be read as a claim about the exact system the hire would build.
