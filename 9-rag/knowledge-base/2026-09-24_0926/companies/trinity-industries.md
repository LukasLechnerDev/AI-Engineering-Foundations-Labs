# Trinity Industries

## Overview

Trinity Industries, Inc. provides railcar products and services in North America. It is headquartered in Dallas, Texas, and reported approximately **6,110 employees** as of December 31, 2025. Its own careers site lists the AI Engineer Associate role in Dallas. ([sec.gov](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/99780/000009978026000014/trn-20251231.htm?utm_source=openai))

## Products and Services

Through its TrinityRail platform, the company leases and manages railcars, manufactures railcars and components, and provides maintenance, modifications, and logistics services. Its portfolio also includes RSI Logistics software and logistics solutions. ([sec.gov](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/99780/000009978026000014/trn-20251231.htm))

## Funding and Ownership

Trinity Industries is **publicly traded**, with common stock listed under the symbol **TRN** on the New York Stock Exchange and NYSE Texas. Venture-funding rounds are not applicable to its current status as a public company. ([sec.gov](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/99780/000009978026000014/trn-20251231.htm?utm_source=openai))

## AI Focus

Trinity has described **Trinsight** as a rail-fleet technology platform that combines telematics, advanced analytics, and machine learning to give customers insights into their railcars and cargo. That is a customer-facing example of its use of AI and data. ([trin.net](https://www.trin.net/investor/press-releases/news-details/2021/TrinityRail-Announces-New-Products-and-Services-Expansion/default.aspx?utm_source=openai))

The **AI Engineer Associate** posting focuses on a different, enterprise-wide use case: building and deploying agentic AI applications for business teams. The role involves large language models and agent frameworks, integrations with internal systems and APIs, and taking applications from prototype to production. Engineers would implement evaluations, secure limits on what agents can access, human approval for higher-risk actions, and production monitoring. The posting also calls for testing, CI/CD, data governance, and collaboration with data and business teams; it names **Databricks** and **Palantir Foundry** as enterprise platforms. These are responsibilities described for the role, not a claim that every proposed application is already deployed. ([trinity.referrals.selectminds.com](https://trinity.referrals.selectminds.com/jobs/ai-engineer-associate-13885?utm_source=openai))
