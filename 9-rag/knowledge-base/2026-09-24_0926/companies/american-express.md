# American Express

## Overview
American Express is a global payments company headquartered in New York City. It employed approximately 76,800 people as of December 31, 2025. Its 2027 AI Engineer I role is with the company’s Enterprise Technology Services team in Sunrise, Florida. ([sec.gov](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/4962/000000496226000080/axp-20251231.htm?utm_source=openai))

## Products and Services
American Express issues credit and charge cards, operates a card network, and provides merchant payment services. Its offerings also include banking, financing, travel, dining, and expense-management services. ([sec.gov](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/4962/000000496226000080/axp-20251231.htm?utm_source=openai))

## Funding and Ownership
American Express is publicly traded on the New York Stock Exchange under **AXP**; it is not a venture-backed startup. Venture funding rounds are therefore not applicable to this company profile. ([sec.gov](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/4962/000000496226000080/axp-20251231.htm?utm_source=openai))

## AI Focus
American Express uses machine learning for fraud detection and is developing generative-AI applications for customer service, personalization, and internal productivity. It has described tools that assist travel counselors, suggest code to engineers, and improve search in its mobile app. Its 2025 annual report says AI-assisted development tools had been rolled out to more than 11,000 engineering professionals. ([americanexpress.com](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/business/american-express-ventures/articles/how-american-express-ventures-is-supporting-generative-ai-innova/?utm_source=openai))

For the **Sunrise AI Engineer I** role, the posting says an engineer may help develop, test, and integrate machine-learning models, data-retrieval pipelines, LLM integrations, and AI agents. The work may also involve preparing and validating data, evaluating models, building inference or prompt-based features, and documenting technical decisions. The posting emphasizes collaboration with product, security, and risk teams so that AI systems are reliable, secure, and appropriately governed; it does not assign the hire to a specific product.
