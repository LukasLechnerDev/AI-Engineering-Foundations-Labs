# Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Nebraska

## Overview

Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Nebraska (BCBSNE) is a health insurer headquartered in Omaha, Nebraska. Workforce data estimates it had **about 1,350 employees** in March 2026. The Omaha-based **Software Engineer – AI** posting is on BCBSNE’s own careers site. ([nebraskablue.com](https://www.nebraskablue.com/Member-Services/Contact-Us?utm_source=openai))

## Products and Services

BCBSNE offers health plans for employers, individuals and families, along with Medicare plans and dental and vision coverage. It also provides members with online tools to review benefits, find doctors and manage claims. ([nebraskablue.com](https://www.nebraskablue.com/?utm_source=openai))

## Funding and Ownership

BCBSNE is **privately held within a mutual insurance holding-company structure**, not a venture-backed startup. Its parent is GoodLife Partners, Inc.; BCBSNE says the reorganization took effect on July 1, 2018. No venture funding rounds were identified in the sources reviewed. ([newsroom.nebraskablue.com](https://newsroom.nebraskablue.com/blue-cross-blue-shield-nebraska-receives-approval-reorganization/))

## AI Focus

BCBSNE says it has a live AI platform whose agents help internal teams reduce manual work, surface insights and improve business processes. The **Software Engineer – AI** role is more specifically focused on a member-facing, multi-agent system. Its posting calls for an engineer to build a LangGraph-based orchestration layer that identifies a member’s intent, routes requests to the appropriate agent and preserves conversational context across handoffs. ([nebraskablue.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com](https://nebraskablue.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/BCBSNE/job/Omaha-NE/Sr-Software-Engineer---AI-Platform_JR101422?utm_source=openai))

The engineer would also define how new agents integrate—including input and output formats, confidence signals and fallbacks—and build retrieval pipelines for benefits documents, formularies and policy content using Azure AI Search. Other stated responsibilities include versioned prompts, evaluation and regression testing, clinical-safety boundaries, protected-health-information handling and consistent responses that meet compliance requirements. The posting identifies Python, Azure OpenAI and Azure Container Apps as part of the target stack. ([nebraskablue.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com](https://nebraskablue.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/BCBSNE/job/Omaha-NE/Software-Engineer_JR101415?utm_source=openai))
