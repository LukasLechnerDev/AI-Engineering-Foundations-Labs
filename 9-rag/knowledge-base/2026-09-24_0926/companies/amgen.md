# Amgen

## Overview
Amgen is a biotechnology company that discovers, develops, manufactures and delivers medicines. It is headquartered in Thousand Oaks, California, and reported approximately **31,500 staff members** as of December 31, 2025. The specified Associate AI Engineer posting is an Amgen role in Thousand Oaks. ([amgen.com](https://www.amgen.com/about?utm_source=openai))

## Products and Services
Amgen’s medicines and development pipeline span general medicine—including cardiovascular and bone health—rare disease, inflammation and oncology. Its marketed products include Repatha, Prolia, Otezla and TEPEZZA. ([amgen.com](https://www.amgen.com/about/therapy-areas?utm_source=openai))

## Funding and Ownership
Amgen is **publicly traded**, not a venture-backed startup or a subsidiary of a larger group. Its common stock trades on Nasdaq under **AMGN**; venture funding rounds are therefore not applicable to this profile. ([sec.gov](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/318154/000031815426000010/amgn-20251231.htm))

## AI Focus
Amgen says it uses AI and machine learning in drug discovery, protein engineering and clinical-trial planning, and sees applications in manufacturing and commercial work. It has also rolled out ChatGPT Enterprise across the company for employee workflows. ([amgen.com](https://www.amgen.com/stories/2025/05/on-the-global-stage-amgens-top-voices-drive-conversations-on-ai-and-innovation))

**For this role specifically**, OI&A means *Obesity Intelligence & Analytics*. The posting places the engineer on its technology operations and data-enablement team, building data-science, software-engineering and generative-AI solutions for commercial and other functions. Likely work includes AI-enabled applications, retrieval-augmented generation, bounded agents, APIs and integrations, plus evaluation and production deployment. The posting also emphasizes security, governance, human review and MLOps/LLMOps—so the work extends beyond prototyping models to making systems reliable in use.
