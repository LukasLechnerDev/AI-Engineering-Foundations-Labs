# iManage

## Overview

iManage is a Chicago-headquartered software company that helps organizations manage documents, email, and institutional knowledge securely. Its customers include law firms and other professional-services organizations. Workforce data estimates **roughly 1,300 employees worldwide** as of March 2026. The Chicago Applied AI Engineer opening is posted by iManage itself, not by a staffing agency. ([imanage.com](https://imanage.com/about/the-company/))

## Products and Services

Its cloud platform includes **iManage Work** for document and email management, **Insight+** for knowledge search, and **Ask iManage** for AI-assisted questions and document analysis. Its security and governance products include Security Policy Manager, Threat Manager, and Disposition Manager. ([imanage.com](https://imanage.com/about/the-company/))

## Funding and Ownership

iManage is privately held. In April 2023, Bain Capital Tech Opportunities announced a **minority growth investment**; iManage described the stake as non-controlling and said its existing management team would continue to lead the company. The cited announcements do not state an investment amount. ([baincapital.com](https://www.baincapital.com/news/imanage-announces-strategic-growth-investment-bain-capital-tech-opportunities))

## AI Focus

iManage builds AI into its knowledge-work platform. **Ask iManage** lets users ask natural-language questions about firm content and receive answers with citations to underlying documents. **Insight+** connects documents with business data and metadata to improve knowledge discovery while maintaining governance controls. The company also describes AI-based threat detection in Threat Manager. ([imanage.com](https://imanage.com/resources/resource-center/news/ask-imanage-delivers-an-ai-powered-search-experience-with-trusted-answers-across-firm-knowledge/))

The **Applied AI Engineer (New or Recent Grad)** posting gives a direct picture of the work: helping develop Ask iManage, document classification and extraction, and other LLM-driven features. It calls for contributions across model development, evaluation, production serving, deployment, and monitoring; writing ML/LLM code and APIs; building evaluations that catch regressions; and creating internal tooling that helps teams ship AI systems safely. This is a production-engineering role on the Applied AI team, working alongside engineers, data scientists, and product stakeholders. ([imanagecom.applytojob.com](https://imanagecom.applytojob.com/apply/r4huAJvEWl/Applied-AI-Engineer-New-Or-Recent-Grad))
