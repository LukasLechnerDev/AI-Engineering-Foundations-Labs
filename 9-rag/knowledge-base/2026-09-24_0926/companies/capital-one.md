# Capital One

## Overview

Capital One is a financial services company headquartered in McLean, Virginia. It reported approximately **76,300 employees worldwide as of December 31, 2025**. Its Staff AI Engineer posting lists San Francisco and remote work among the location options. ([sec.gov](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/927628/000092762826000024/cof-20251231.htm?utm_source=openai))

## Products and Services

Capital One offers credit and debit cards, deposit accounts, auto loans, other consumer lending, and banking and treasury-management services for businesses. It also operates digital banking channels and a payments network following its acquisition of Discover. ([sec.gov](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/927628/000092762826000024/cof-20251231.htm?utm_source=openai))

## Funding and Ownership

Capital One is **publicly traded on the NYSE under COF**, rather than venture-backed. It completed its acquisition of Discover in May 2025 and its acquisition of business-payments company Brex in April 2026. These were acquisitions, not funding rounds for Capital One. ([capitalone.com](https://www.capitalone.com/about/newsroom/capital-one-completes-acquisition-of-discover/?utm_source=openai))

## AI Focus

Capital One says it builds proprietary AI as well as applying machine learning to personalized customer experiences. Its published examples include a multi-agent car-buying assistant, a transformer-based recommendation system, and a retrieval-augmented generative AI tool that helps employees answer customer questions. ([capitalone.com](https://www.capitalone.com/tech/ai/?utm_source=openai))

The advertised **Staff AI Engineer** role sits on the *Intelligent Foundations and Experiences* team, which builds AI models and platforms for use across the company. The posting describes work on foundation-model training, LLM inference, agents and multi-agent workflows, similarity search, guardrails, evaluation, governance, and observability. It also calls for optimizing production AI systems for cost, latency, throughput, and scale, and setting shared architecture and deployment standards. The engineer would work with researchers, product managers, and other engineers on tools for employees and customer-facing experiences. ([capitalonecareers.com](https://www.capitalonecareers.com/job/san-francisco/staff-ai-engineer-remote-eligible/1732/101053670256))
