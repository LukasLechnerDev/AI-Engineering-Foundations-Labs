# RA Capital Management

## Overview

RA Capital Management is a Boston-based investment manager focused on healthcare, life sciences, and planetary health. It has **more than 200 team members**. The AI Engineer opening is with RA Capital itself, not an unnamed client or one of its portfolio companies. ([racap.com](https://www.racap.com/about))

## Products and Services

RA Capital provides investment capital from company formation and seed rounds through venture financing, IPOs, and public markets. It also helps build companies through its Raven healthcare incubator and uses its TechAtlas research platform to evaluate scientific opportunities. ([racap.com](https://www.racap.com/invest-with-ra-capital))

## Funding and Ownership

RA Capital is a **privately held investment manager**, not a publicly traded company. Its fundraises should not be confused with venture funding for the management firm: it announced the **$120 million final close of its inaugural Planetary Health Fund in July 2025**. The firm reported **more than $14.4 billion in assets under management as of December 31, 2025**. ([lei.bloomberg.com](https://lei.bloomberg.com/leis/view/549300I8JEUGT6FRA214?utm_source=openai))

## AI Focus

RA Capital’s TechAtlas platform organizes proprietary scientific and market research to support investment decisions. Its Boston AI Engineer posting describes a role building AI and machine-learning systems to identify and evaluate investment opportunities in biomedical technologies, with work on proprietary datasets that could also inform decisions about medical and climate technologies. ([racap.com](https://www.racap.com/invest-with-ra-capital))

The engineer would develop and deploy end-to-end AI applications; build deep-learning models for time-series, business, and image data; enhance large language models trained on proprietary research; and create real-time prediction systems and full-stack interfaces. The posting names PyTorch, AWS SageMaker and Bedrock, and container orchestration among relevant technologies. These are **stated responsibilities and requirements**, rather than a claim that every proposed system is already deployed. ([job-boards.greenhouse.io](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/racapitalmanagementllc/jobs/4563736008))
