# WRITER

## Overview

WRITER is a San Francisco-headquartered enterprise AI company, not a recruiting agency. It provides a platform for companies to build and use AI agents grounded in their own data. Its LinkedIn company profile lists **201–500 employees**. WRITER’s own job posting identifies an AI engineer role available in San Francisco. ([writer.com](https://writer.com/company/about/?utm_source=openai))

## Products and Services

WRITER’s products include WRITER Agent, which plans and executes work across connected data and tools; Knowledge Graph, which retrieves information from company sources; and AI Studio, where teams build applications and agents and manage access, guardrails, and monitoring. ([writer.com](https://writer.com/product/writer-agent/?utm_source=openai))

## Funding and Ownership

WRITER is **privately held and venture-backed**. It announced a $100 million Series B in 2023 and a $200 million Series C in November 2024, the latter at a reported $1.9 billion valuation. Series C investors included Premji Invest, Radical Ventures, and ICONIQ Growth. ([linkedin.com](https://www.linkedin.com/company/getwriter/?utm_source=openai))

## AI Focus

WRITER develops language models and the systems that turn them into enterprise agents. Its August 2026 Palmyra X6 release focused on marketing and revenue workflows; WRITER also described upgrades to its agent system for planning, tool use, task delegation, and more reliable, lower-cost execution. Its platform combines models with retrieval from company knowledge, permissions, safety controls, and observability. ([writer.com](https://writer.com/blog/palmyra-x6-major-harness-release/?utm_source=openai))

For the **San Francisco AI engineer role**, WRITER says the work includes designing and deploying scalable AI applications, building agents and AI-powered features, integrating them with its platform, and evaluating emerging AI techniques. The posting also calls out responsible-AI work such as bias detection, explainability, and safety alignment. ([jobs.ashbyhq.com](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/writer/098a1bc6-ea8f-489a-945e-f94744c9ea22?utm_source=openai))
