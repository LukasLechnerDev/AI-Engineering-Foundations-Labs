# Realtor.com

## Overview

Realtor.com is a real estate marketplace operated by Move, Inc. It helps people search for homes and connects them with real estate professionals. Its headquarters is in Austin, Texas. Its Built In company profile lists roughly **1,400 employees**. ([mediaroom.realtor.com](https://mediaroom.realtor.com/2025-02-06-Realtor-com-R-Makes-Austin%2C-Texas-Its-New-Headquarters))

## Products and Services

Its website and apps offer property listings, home-search tools, market information, and resources for buyers, sellers, renters, and homeowners. It also offers real estate professionals consumer connections and branding solutions. Move’s other brands include Avail, a rental-management platform, and ListHub, a listing-distribution platform. ([mediaroom.realtor.com](https://mediaroom.realtor.com/2025-02-06-Realtor-com-R-Makes-Austin%2C-Texas-Its-New-Headquarters))

## Funding and Ownership

Realtor.com is part of Move, which is **majority-owned by publicly traded News Corp**; it is not a standalone public company. News Corp completed its acquisition of Move in November 2014, following a deal announced at approximately **$950 million**. REA Group subsequently acquired a 20% stake in Move. ([realtor.com](https://www.realtor.com/about/leadership?utm_source=openai))

## AI Focus

Realtor.com uses AI in consumer products, including natural-language home search and **RealAssist**, a conversational real estate assistant developed with Google technology. Its engineering team says image models and natural-language processing enrich listings with searchable property attributes; machine-learning models also generate personalized home recommendations. ([techblog.realtor.com](https://techblog.realtor.com/search-how-youd-say-it-building-ai-powered-search-at-realtor-com/))

The **Austin AI Engineer** posting is specifically for the AI Integrations team’s *safety layer* for RealAssist. The engineer would build and tune LLM-based classifiers for fair-housing compliance and content moderation; curate labeled evaluation datasets; measure precision, recall, and other safety metrics; and test for prompt injection and jailbreaks. The role also covers cloud guardrails, automated regression checks, Terraform-managed infrastructure, and collaboration with product, engineering, and legal/compliance teams before AI features launch. ([builtinaustin.com](https://www.builtinaustin.com/job/ai-engineer/11341877?utm_source=openai))
