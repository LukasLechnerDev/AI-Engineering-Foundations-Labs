# Thomson Reuters

## Overview
Thomson Reuters is an AI and technology company that provides trusted content and workflow automation for professionals. It is headquartered in Toronto, Ontario, Canada, and its latest company materials describe it as having about 26,000 employees. ([thomsonreuters.com](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en/about-us))

## Products and Services
Its main businesses are Legal, Tax and Accounting, Risk and Fraud, Trade and Supply, and News and Media (Reuters). It provides online and print information, software, decision tools, and services for legal, tax, accounting, compliance, government, and media customers. ([thomsonreuters.com](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en/about-us))

## Funding and Ownership
Thomson Reuters is a public company listed on TSX/Nasdaq under TRI. Its 2025 annual report was filed on March 5, 2026, and its corporate structure materials identify Thomson Reuters Corporation as incorporated in Ontario, Canada. ([thomsonreuters.com](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en/press-releases/2026/march/thomson-reuters-files-2025-annual-report?utm_source=openai))

## AI Focus
Thomson Reuters describes itself as an AI and technology company and says it uses AI to support trusted content, workflow automation, and better decision-making. It has internal AI tools such as Open Arena, which is available to all employees and supports multiple LLM providers, and the company says it is integrating GenAI into flagship solutions and training employees on AI use. A Generative AI & Machine Learning Engineer in McLean, VA would likely work on applied ML/GenAI systems for professional workflows, responsible AI, and product features in high-stakes domains. ([thomsonreuters.com](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en/about-us))
