# Victra-Verizon Authorized Retailer

## Overview
Victra is a privately held Verizon Authorized Retailer, founded in 1996 and independently operated; it is not owned by Verizon. Its corporate headquarters/Store Support Center is in Durham, North Carolina. Victra says it is the largest exclusive Verizon Authorized Retailer in the U.S. and has more than 1,800 stores across all 50 states. ([victra.com](https://victra.com/frequently-asked-questions/?utm_source=openai))

## Products and Services
Victra sells Verizon wireless products and services through its retail stores and customer-support channels. Its site mentions help with billing and payments, account management, plans and add-ons, and troubleshooting, and its team page says it helps customers with devices and plans. ([victra.com](https://victra.com/contact/?utm_source=openai))

## Funding and Ownership
Victra is a private company backed by private equity and operated independently as an authorized retailer. No reliable public funding-round details were found in the search results. ([victra.com](https://victra.com/frequently-asked-questions/?utm_source=openai))
