# Create AWS Root Account

1. Head over to https://aws.amazon.com
2. Click "Create Account"
3. Enter root user email address and AWS Account name
4. Enter email verification code
5. Choose a really strong and unique password and store it in a safe place (password manager)
6. Chose "Personal" for the account type
7. Enter your address information
8. Enter your credit card information
9. Confirm identity with your phone number
10. Choose "Basic Support - Free" for the support plan
11. Click on "Complete Signup" and you are done 🥳 

The next step is to click on "Go to AWS Management Console"
