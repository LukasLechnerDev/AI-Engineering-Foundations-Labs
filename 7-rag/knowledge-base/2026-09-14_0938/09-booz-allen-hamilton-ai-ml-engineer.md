# AI/ML Engineer

**Job Number: R0246722**
 AI/ML Engineer
   

  

**The Opportunity:**
 As an experienced engineer, you know that building reliable and scalable AI systems requires far more than just model development—it demands strong software engineering practices, automation, and seamless integration across complex environments. Your ability to design and maintain robust AI and ML pipelines, automate workflows, and integrate AI capabilities into production systems makes you an essential part of delivering resilient, mission\-focused solutions. We need your technical expertise and problem‑solving mindset to support the development and operationalization of AI capabilities that directly enhance mission performance and decision‑making. As an AI and ML engineer on our national security team, you’ll build, test, deploy, and operate software end‑to‑end AI pipelines that keep mission systems running securely and efficiently.
   

  

 Join us. The world can’t wait.
   

  

**You Have:**
* Experience with data science, ML engineering, software engineering, data research, or data analytics
* Experience building and deploying AI and ML solutions into production
* Experience implementing document RAG and Graph RAG solutions
* Experience building applications leveraging LLMs for automation tasks such as text summarization or customized reports, including with multi\-agent solutions
* Experience adapting and fine\-tuning open\-source models
* Experience with object\-oriented Python programming and best practices
* Experience with containerization technology such as Docker, Helm, or Kubernetes
* Ability to create solutions within a collaborative, cross\-functional team environment
* Ability to obtain a TS/SCI clearance
* Bachelor's degree and 3\+ years of experience with AI and ML, or 6\+ years of experience with AI and ML in lieu of a degree


**Nice If You Have:**
* Experience with databases such as Qdrant, Milvus, or Elasticsearch
* Experience with Elasticsearch index
* Experience with relational databases such as Postgres
* Experience with on\-prem compute
* TS/SCI clearance
