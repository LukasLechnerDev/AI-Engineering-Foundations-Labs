# OpenAI

## Overview
OpenAI is an AI research and product company founded in 2015, originally as a nonprofit, with its mission to ensure that artificial general intelligence benefits all of humanity. Its headquarters is not explicitly stated in the sources I found, but the role is listed in San Francisco, California, and OpenAI’s careers page is based there. OpenAI says it has “over 3,000 employees.” ([openai.com](https://openai.com/our-structure/))

## Products and Services
OpenAI’s main products and services include ChatGPT, ChatGPT Business, ChatGPT Enterprise, ChatGPT for Education, Codex, and the OpenAI API platform. For businesses, OpenAI offers solutions for functions like sales, marketing, engineering, finance, data analytics, and research, with use cases such as coding, content creation, agents, and data analysis. ([openai.com](https://openai.com/our-structure/))

## Funding and Ownership
OpenAI is now structured as a public benefit corporation, OpenAI Group PBC, with the OpenAI Foundation controlling it. OpenAI says the Foundation holds a 26% equity stake in OpenAI Group, Microsoft holds roughly 27%, and the remaining 47% is held by current and former employees and investors. OpenAI was founded as a nonprofit and later created a for-profit subsidiary in 2019 to scale research and deployment. ([openai.com](https://openai.com/our-structure/))

## AI Focus
OpenAI builds and deploys advanced AI models and products for both consumers and enterprises. The GTM Growth Engineering role appears tied to applied AI and go-to-market growth work, and OpenAI’s business materials emphasize using AI across departments, connecting customer data, supporting coding and research workflows, and deploying AI securely with enterprise controls. An engineer in this area would likely work on AI-powered growth systems, internal tooling, product surfaces, and integrations that help bring OpenAI’s models into customer workflows. ([openai.com](https://openai.com/careers/search/?q=growth&utm_source=openai))
