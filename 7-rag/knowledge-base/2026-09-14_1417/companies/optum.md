# Optum

## Overview
Optum is a health services company and a division of UnitedHealth Group. It says it connects doctor visits, mental health, and specialty care across clinics, home, and online, and it is headquartered in Eden Prairie, Minnesota. Optum’s site says it has about 300,000 employees worldwide. The AI/ML Engineer role you specified is listed for Minnetonka, Minnesota, which matches Optum’s Minnesota presence and its UnitedHealth Group careers posting for that job. ([optum.com](https://www.optum.com/en/about-us.html))

## Products and Services
Optum’s main offerings span healthcare services and operations, including Optum Health, Optum Insight, and Optum Rx. Its business pages also show services in behavioral health, employee assistance programs, nurse line services, dialysis networks, transplant programs, claims review, home-based care, revenue integrity, and other healthcare workflow and administration solutions. ([unitedhealthgroup.com](https://www.unitedhealthgroup.com/content/dam/UHG/PDF/investors/2024/UNH-Q4-2024-Form-10-K.pdf))

## Funding and Ownership
Optum is not a standalone public company; it is part of UnitedHealth Group, which is publicly traded. UnitedHealth Group’s 10-K describes Optum as one of its two complementary businesses, alongside UnitedHealthcare. Optum’s own site also describes it as “A UnitedHealth Group company.” ([unitedhealthgroup.com](https://www.unitedhealthgroup.com/content/dam/UHG/PDF/investors/2024/UNH-Q4-2024-Form-10-K.pdf))

## AI Focus
Optum’s public materials show a strong focus on data, analytics, and AI in healthcare. Its business site highlights AI products that use natural language processing, and it explicitly references using AI while pairing it with evidence-based criteria and clinician insight for trustworthy decisions. The job posting for Senior AI/ML Engineer says Optum Tech uses advanced data analytics and AI to solve complex healthcare problems, so an AI/ML engineer there would likely work on healthcare data products, automation, predictive models, or NLP-driven tools. ([business.optum.com](https://business.optum.com/en/all-solutions.html))
