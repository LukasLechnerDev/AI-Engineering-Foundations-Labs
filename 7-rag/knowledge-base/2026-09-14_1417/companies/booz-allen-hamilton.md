# Booz Allen Hamilton

## Overview
Booz Allen Hamilton is a U.S.-based technology and consulting company headquartered in McLean, Virginia. Its work spans civil, defense, and national security missions, and its latest company fact sheet says it has about 31,600 employees across 23 countries. ([boozallen.com](https://www.boozallen.com/content/dam/home/docs/corp/booz-allen-hamilton-fact-sheet.pdf?source=content_type%3Areact%7Cfirst_level_url%3Aarticle%7Csection%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link))

## Products and Services
Booz Allen provides consulting, analysis, and engineering services, with a strong focus on technology solutions for government and some commercial clients. Its AI offering includes AI-ready enterprise strategy, data for AI, AI innovation, and AI engineering; the company also highlights vision AI, AI for cybersecurity, and other mission-focused applications. ([boozallen.com](https://www.boozallen.com/about.html))

## Funding and Ownership
Booz Allen Hamilton Holding Corporation is a public company and the parent of Booz Allen Hamilton Inc. The company is listed on the NYSE under BAH, and it operates as a publicly traded holding company rather than a venture-backed startup. Its materials also note strategic investment in advanced technology, including a $300 million ventures fund and $3 billion invested in advanced tech and innovation over the past decade. ([sec.gov](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1443646/000162828026037521/bah-20260331.htm))

## AI Focus
Booz Allen emphasizes end-to-end AI services for federal and commercial missions, including data and model management, AI engineering, platform management, and security. The company says its aiSSEMBLE approach standardizes AI design, development, and delivery, with templates for data/ML pipelines, ML lifecycle tooling, and deployment support such as container and Kubernetes configuration. An AI/ML Engineer there would likely work on model development, MLOps, deployment, and mission-critical AI systems, especially for secure and regulated environments. ([boozallen.com](https://www.boozallen.com/expertise/artificial-intelligence/ai-solutions.html))
