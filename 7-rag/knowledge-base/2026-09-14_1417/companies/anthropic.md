# Anthropic

## Overview
Anthropic is an AI safety and research company that builds Claude. It is headquartered in San Francisco, California, and Anthropic says it has a collaborative team of researchers, engineers, policy experts, business leaders, and operators. Reliable public workforce data puts the company at roughly 3,830 employees worldwide as of March 2026, though this is an external estimate rather than an official headcount. ([anthropic.com](https://www.anthropic.com/company?utm_source=openai))

## Products and Services
Anthropic’s main product is Claude, its AI assistant/model family. The company also offers Claude-related enterprise and developer products, plus applied-AI, partnerships, and customer-success services around deploying Claude in organizations. Anthropic’s careers pages and job listings show a large GTM organization supporting sales, partnerships, customer success, and technical enablement for Claude and Claude Platform. ([anthropic.com](https://www.anthropic.com/careers?utm_source=openai))

## Funding and Ownership
Anthropic is a public benefit corporation and a private, venture-backed company. In 2026 it announced a $30 billion Series G at a $380 billion post-money valuation, and later a $65 billion Series H at a $965 billion valuation, with major investors including large venture and institutional firms. Anthropic also says it publicly discloses its funding rounds and investor participation. ([anthropic.com](https://www.anthropic.com/transparency/voluntary-commitments?utm_source=openai))

## AI Focus
Anthropic focuses on building reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems, with safety and research at the center of its work. For a Staff AI Engineer, GTM Claudification role, the work would likely involve adapting Claude for go-to-market use cases, improving internal and customer-facing workflows, and helping sales/partnership teams scale Claude adoption with technical tooling and automation. Anthropic’s current hiring mix around GTM, technical enablement, customer success, and Claude Platform supports that inference. ([anthropic.com](https://www.anthropic.com/company/leadership?utm_source=openai))
