# Aderant

## Overview
Aderant is a legal software company that provides business management and practice-of-law solutions for law firms. It is headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, and its website states it has 700+ employees around the globe. Aderant operates as a business unit and subsidiary of Roper Technologies. ([aderant.com](https://www.aderant.com/about-aderant/?utm_source=openai))

## Products and Services
Aderant’s core offerings include business management and practice-of-law software for law firms, with products spanning financial management, matter management, billing, compliance, analytics, cloud platforms, and workflow tools. Its site highlights products and platforms such as Expert Sierra, Stridyn, and MADDI, along with newer AI agent capabilities through Agent Center. ([aderant.com](https://www.aderant.com/aderant-firm/?utm_source=openai))

## Funding and Ownership
Aderant is not publicly traded on its own; it is part of Roper Technologies, a public company listed on Nasdaq. Aderant previously had private equity ownership, and Roper acquired Aderant in 2015. ([aderant.com](https://www.aderant.com/about-aderant/?utm_source=openai))

## AI Focus
Aderant is actively building AI into both its products and internal engineering workflows. Recent company materials describe AI-native capabilities in Stridyn, the MADDI virtual associate, and the Agent Center, which uses AI agents for workflows like collections, compliance, rates, and forecasting. The company also says it uses AI to improve engineering productivity and accelerate development while keeping enterprise-grade controls and oversight. An AI Engineer in Atlanta would likely work on agentic workflow automation, AI product integration, data/context orchestration, and tooling for legal and financial operations. ([aderant.com](https://www.aderant.com/news-pr/aderant-launches-early-access-to-ai-agents/?utm_source=openai))
