# CoreWeave

## Overview
CoreWeave is an AI-focused cloud infrastructure company that provides a platform purpose-built for scaling and accelerating generative AI workloads. It is headquartered in Livingston, New Jersey, and was founded in 2017. CoreWeave is a public company that completed its Nasdaq listing in March 2025. For employee count, the most reliable current public sources I found did not give a precise number on CoreWeave’s own site; LinkedIn indicates the company has employees, but I could not verify an exact current total from a primary source, so I’m not stating a number here. ([investors.coreweave.com](https://investors.coreweave.com/overview/default.aspx?utm_source=openai))

## Products and Services
CoreWeave’s main offering is its cloud platform for AI workloads, including infrastructure and tools to deploy, scale, monitor, and run GenAI systems. Its site describes the company as a “cloud purpose-built for scaling, supporting, and accelerating GenAI,” and its investor materials position it as “The Essential Cloud for AI.” ([investors.coreweave.com](https://investors.coreweave.com/overview/default.aspx?utm_source=openai))

## Funding and Ownership
CoreWeave is a public company listed on Nasdaq under ticker CRWV. It was originally formed in 2017 as a Delaware LLC and later converted to a Delaware corporation. I found reliable confirmation of the IPO/public listing, but not a clean primary-source summary of earlier funding rounds in the material I reviewed, so I’m not adding unsupported round details. ([investors.coreweave.com](https://investors.coreweave.com/overview/default.aspx?utm_source=openai))

## AI Focus
CoreWeave builds and operates infrastructure specifically for GenAI and AI workloads, so an AI Engineer there would likely work on systems that support model training, inference, orchestration, performance, reliability, and developer tooling for large-scale AI deployments. The company’s own messaging emphasizes accelerating AI at scale and helping customers run demanding AI workloads efficiently. ([coreweave.com](https://coreweave.com/about-us?utm_source=openai))
