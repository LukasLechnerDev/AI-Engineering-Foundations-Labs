# Univera Healthcare

## Overview
Univera Healthcare is a nonprofit health plan serving the eight counties of Western New York, headquartered at 205 Park Club Lane in Buffalo, NY. It is part of The Lifetime Healthcare Companies, which says it provides health insurance coverage to about 1.5 million members and employs more than 4,000 New Yorkers. ([news.univerahealthcare.com](https://news.univerahealthcare.com/about-us/company-facts))

## Products and Services
Univera Healthcare offers health insurance coverage and related care-management services for individuals, employers, and Medicare/Medicaid members. Its public materials highlight coverage plans, access to provider networks and pharmacies, utilization management, care management, disease management, and behavioral health management. ([news.univerahealthcare.com](https://news.univerahealthcare.com/about-us/corporate-overview))

## Funding and Ownership
Univera Healthcare is a nonprofit organization and is part of The Lifetime Healthcare Companies, also described as a nonprofit organization. I did not find reliable public information about outside venture funding or notable funding rounds, which is consistent with its nonprofit structure. ([news.univerahealthcare.com](https://news.univerahealthcare.com/about-us/company-facts))

## AI Focus
Univera Healthcare’s job posting for an AI Engineer in Buffalo says the role is part of a collaborative team that develops machine learning and AI models to solve business challenges and improve member health outcomes. The posting also mentions identifying high-impact AI opportunities with stakeholders. Company materials also point to AI use in utilization management, where AI is presented as a way to simplify administrative work and free up more time for patient care. ([linkedin.com](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/ai-engineer-i-ii-at-univera-healthcare-4425855810?utm_source=openai))
