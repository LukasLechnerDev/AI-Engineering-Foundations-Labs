# Founding AI Engineer

Our client is a well\-capitalized, venture\-backed startup (founded 2025\) building an AI platform for the commercial insurance industry. Their product automates the document\-heavy workflows that slow insurance professionals down, in a large market that is only beginning to adopt AI. You would be one of the first engineers on a small team, shaping the product from the ground up.


Founded 2025 · \~8 people · Industry: AI, Insurance

**The Role**
------------


As a founding engineer, you’ll build the AI agents and workflows that power the product and own customer\-facing features end\-to\-end, working closely with the founding team in a fast\-moving environment.

**What you’ll be doing**

* Build AI agents and workflows for real insurance use cases
* Ship customer\-facing product features end\-to\-end
* Iterate quickly alongside the founding team in an ambiguous, high\-growth setting
* Use modern AI coding tools as a core part of your daily workflow

**Tech stack:** Python, React, AI coding tools

**Requirements**
----------------

* 2 to 5 years of experience as a full stack engineer with exposure to AI\-powered applications
* A recent track record (within the last 1 to 2 years) of shipping customer\-facing products end\-to\-end
* Hands\-on, daily use of AI coding tools such as Claude Code, Codex, or similar
* Experience at an early\-stage startup (Seed to Series B) during a high\-growth phase
* Strong Python skills
* A computer science degree or equivalent technical foundation
* A language\-agnostic mindset, comfortable picking up new tools and switching between languages as needed

**Nice to Haves**
-----------------

* Prior experience in an AI Product Engineer or Applied AI Engineer role
* Exposure to React

**Why Join**
------------

* A true founding\-engineer seat with day\-one ownership of product
* Ship to real customers immediately in a large, fast\-growing market
* Well\-capitalized and venture\-backed, with an experienced founder
